# Seasonal month artwork

Twelve individual backgrounds for the staff school calendar. Generated using the built-in image generation tool; the approved day artwork and school logo were the visual references. Converted to 1200×400 WebP for delivery. UI crops and responsive sizing are controlled by CSS; month and season labels remain live text. The palette derives from MONTH_PALETTES, darkened behind white text. Header heights remain 48.6px desktop and 46px mobile.

## Shared generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one polished painterly seasonal banner background for De Telescoop school calendar by transforming the reference artwork into the specified month scene. Reference 1 is the approved painting style, reference 2 is school identity. Keep the refined hand-painted oil texture, golden brass telescope, dreamlike René Magritte surrealism, luminous sculpted clouds, beautiful restrained detail. This is a distinct month variant, not a copy of the same scene. Exact aspect ratio 3:1 horizontal. Full bleed art, NO text, NO letters, NO month name, NO frame, NO white margin, NO UI. Layout for a shallow header: the left 50% is calm subtly textured dark version of the specified monthly hue, not navy unless winter, blending organically into the painting. Place the telescope and the one seasonal landmark/motif on the RIGHT half, primarily around x=75%, y=50%; their most recognizable parts must fit within the central horizontal band y=30%-70% so a shallow banner crop remains beautiful. Do not put tiny details everywhere. A few beautifully painted objects. Keep the outer right edge free of important details. Golden telescope remains recognizable at small scale. Month palette is dominant throughout, allowing cream clouds and gold accents.
